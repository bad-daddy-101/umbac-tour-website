Why UMBAC Tour Exists

Not just another bracket. UMBAC Tour preserves the matches, rivalries, team names, player moments, and stories that local competition usually loses after the stream ends.

Broadcast First

Matches are presented with custom graphics, live casting featuring Killershin, standings, overlays, and storylines built around the teams.

Team Legacy

Teams are remembered beyond a single match night through archived results, placements, posters, and championship history.

Community Stage

The local esports community is provided with a platform to showcase their skills and to write their own legends.
